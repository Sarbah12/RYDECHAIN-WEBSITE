Why RydeChain

Built so the money goes where it's earned

Most ride platforms sit between you and your driver, holding funds and taking a cut on the way through. RydeChain removes the middle.

Direct wallet-to-wallet

ADA moves from the passenger's Cardano wallet to the driver's and is verified on-chain before the trip begins. The platform never holds keys or funds.

Transparent pricing

The fare is shown upfront and confirmed before you set off. No hidden fees, no surge surprises, no price that changes once you're moving.

Fast, low-cost settlement

Cardano's low fees keep payment overhead to a fraction of card processing, and settlement confirms in minutes — no payout delays for drivers.

Driver autonomy

Drivers aren't just order-takers. They publish their own trips with custom routes, stops, schedules, seat counts and their own ADA pricing.

Reputation you can verify

Drivers and passengers build reputation through ratings on completed trips, with records anchored to verifiable on-chain payment transactions.

Tailored to Africa

Fares in ADA with GHS equivalents, cash supported alongside crypto, and weather-aware pricing tuned to local conditions. Starting with Accra.

How it works

Four steps, no middleman

From request to settlement, every stage is visible to both sides of the trip.

Request or browse

Request a ride on demand with an upfront estimate, or browse driver-published trips that fit your schedule and budget.

Get matched

Smart dispatch matches you to nearby drivers in progressive waves, prioritising proximity and availability.

Confirm the fare

You see the exact fare in ADA with its GHS equivalent and confirm it before the trip starts — it cannot change mid-ride.

Pay direct

Pay in ADA from your own wallet — confirmed on-chain to the driver's address — or settle in cash on arrival.

For drivers

Run your day, your route, your price

Drivers have full control over their trips. Beyond accepting on-demand requests, you can publish your own — and keep the full fare your rider pays.

  • Set your routes. Choose starting points, intermediate stops and destinations.
  • Set your price. Publish trips at your own rate in ADA per seat.
  • Set your schedule. Pick trip times and seat availability to match your day.
  • Get paid immediately. Fares land in your wallet directly — no platform float, no payout delay.

Payments

Pay the way that works for you

Riders choose their payment method trip by trip — essential in a market where that flexibility matters.

ADA on Cardano

Straight from your wallet to the driver's, verified on-chain before departure. Link your wallet with an optional DID reference — private keys are never stored.

Cash on arrival

Fully supported, first-class. Choose cash at booking and settle with the driver when you arrive — no wallet required to ride.

Mobile money Planned

MoMo integration is on the roadmap as an additional settlement rail, to meet riders where they already pay.

Where RydeChain is heading

Direct transfer works today. Next comes trustless settlement and portable, verifiable trust.
